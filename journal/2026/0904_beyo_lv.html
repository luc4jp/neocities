Watching Kurumi Takase’s Farewell Concert Live from the Cinema & My Thoughts on BEYOOOOONDS

高瀬くるみさんの卒コンライブビューイングに行ってきた話とビヨの話

Best seat in the venue, let's go. 一番いい席に座れました

I went to the cinema to watch the live broadcast of Kurumi Takase’s farewell concert from BEYOOOOONDS at Yokohama Arena. The pre-show featured a video message from Rika Shimakura, who is currently studying abroad—I was already on the edge of tears! Then the show kicked off with "High Tension," and Kurumi appeared right under the spotlight. Realizing "She won't be here starting tomorrow" made the tears flow uncontrollably. Also, Momohime Kiyono got a pixie haircut!!! The girls sitting next to me and I all went "WOOOOOAH!!" together, it was so hyped! BEYOOOOONDSの高瀬くるみさんの横浜アリーナ卒業公演のライブビューイングを見に映画館へ行きました。開演前アナウンスで留学中の島倉りかちゃんからのビデオメッセージがあり、かなり泣きそうになっていたところに『ハイ！テンション』からのスタートで、くるみんがピンスポで出てきて、「明日からもう、この子いないのかよ」と思ったら涙が止まらなかった。てか、清野桃々姫さんがショートカットにしてて！！！隣の席のお姉さん達と一緒に「ふぉおおおおお！！」ってなってぶち上がりました。

So glad Kurumi made it in time for her final MV before farewell. くるみん出演最後のMV！間に合ってよかった

The gasps of surprise when the new members appeared in the intermission VTR felt incredible! Both Yokohama Arena and the cinema theater gasped "EEEEEH?!" in unison. Then, during "That's LIFE!", the new members joined the performance. Seeing all the white glowsticks—likely from fans of Hana Kojima's former group—and everyone in the arena smiling brought me to tears again. 幕間VTRに新メン達が出てきた瞬間の驚きの声はかなり気持ちよかった。横アリもライビュ会場も一体になって「えええ〜〜〜〜！！」って。そしたら『That's LIFE！』で新メンバーがパフォーマンスに参加して。小島はなさんの前グループファンの方々と思しき、白のペンラがたくさん、そして横アリにいる人たちみんな笑顔なのが映ってもうだめだった。涙。

Joining the performance before even getting a self-introduction—absolute power move. 自己紹介の前にパフォーマンスに参加という英断

I thought DJ KOO would only appear in the VTR, so seeing him physically show up for "SaiKOO DE DANCE" blew my mind! During the final chorus of "Nippon no D・N・A!", the camera pulled back just like in the music video. Memories of everything flooded back, and my vision got so blurry with tears that I could barely see the screen. DJ KOOさんはVTRでの登場だけだと思ってたからまさかの『最KOO DE DANCE』で出てきてブチ上がった。『ニッポンノD・N・A！』のラスサビでカメラが引いて、MVと同じ感じになるところで今までのことぶわーって思い出して、「なんでこんなに泣いてるんだ、スクリーンを見たいのに」って涙で滲んで大変で。

An earthquake hit Yokohama that day, but everyone was too busy hyping to notice. この日、横浜は地震があったけど会場がぶち上がりすぎてて誰も気づかなかったらしい

Honestly, for farewell solo songs, covers of other groups' tracks are totally fine. Yet Kurumi chose "Nobishiro ~Beyond the World~". It really showed how deeply she loves BEYOOOOONDS. During the song, members gave their messages, and Minami Okamura's words hit particularly hard. Finally, during "Arigato Beat", the audience fan calls were overwhelmingly loud. At the very end, we even got to do the iconic "Tension?" "TAKASEEEEE!!" call-and-response. What an absolute masterpiece of a farewell concert. I walked out of the cinema in tears—feeling like my soul had left my body. What a day. そして卒業ソロなんて、別に他のグループの歌でいいじゃないですか。なのにくるみんは『伸びしろ～Beyond the World～』で。この人どれだけBEYOOOOONDSを愛してくれてんだよと。歌の間に、メンバーからのメッセージがあって、なかでも岡村美波さんにグッときた。最後『ありがとビート』で、お名前コールがすごくて、ヲタクのコールが響き渡ってた。最後の最後に「テンショーン？」「「「たかせーーーー！！」」」もできて。もうなんて最高な卒コンなんだと。泣きながらそそくさと映画館を出ましたね。魂が抜けたような、そんな日でした。。

The previous tour final at the Budokan was held right after they announced the new member auditions. Because of that timing, both the members and the fans felt this intense, desperate drive—a sense that "this is our absolute last big-venue show with just the founding members. 前回のツアーファイナルの武道館は、新メンバーオーディションをやります！という発表の後で、結成時のメンバーのみで行う、大きめの箱でのライブはこれで最後という気持ちがあってかなり気合いが入ってた。

BEYO's Budokan tradition: the manual uncut recap medley. 武道館恒例の人力ノーカット振り返りメドレー

But then, the audition ended with "no eligible candidates," and nobody was added after all. Having put so much emotional weight into that Budokan concert, I couldn't help but feel a bit deflated, like the rug had been pulled out from under us. As a fan, it felt selfish to think, "Wait, so what was all that emotional buildup at Budokan even for?" After that, agency-wide auditions kept going, but there was no sign of anyone joining BEYOOOOONDS. To make matters worse, Itsuki Nishimura—a Hello Pro Kenshuusei famous for being a huge BEYOOOOONDS fan—ended up debuting in Tsubaki Factory instead. It left me quietly worrying about where the group was heading. Reading the members' blog posts back then was an absolute emotional rollercoaster. They were being tossed around by circumstances completely out of their control. On top of that, they clearly felt the weight of the fans' intense desire for them to "just stay the way they are." So when the sudden announcement of new members finally dropped, it came as a total shock. At the same time, I felt this deep sense of relief knowing BEYOOOOONDS’ music would continue to be carried forward. でもその後のオーディションは「該当者なし」で結局増員しなかった。気合いを入れた分、なんだか空回ったというか肩透かしを食らった気分というか、「あの武道館に懸けた思いはなんだったんだ」みたいに、ファンとしてかなり勝手だけど思ってしまって。それから事務所全体でのオーディションは継続して行われてたけど、増員する気配はなかったし、ビヨのヲタクで有名だった研修生の西村乙輝ちゃんはつばきファクトリーでデビューしたしで、どうなるんだろうってうっすら心配してた。当時のメンバーのブログを読むと感情がぐちゃぐちゃになるんだよな。メンバーだけではどうしようもない事情に翻弄されてるというか、何よりファンたちの「このままでいてほしい」みたいな気持ちもかなりメンバーに伝わってたし。だからいきなりの加入発表はびっくりした。でもBEYOOOOONDSの歌がこれからも継がれていくんだっていう安心感はあった。

Still, I couldn’t help but ask myself: "What kind of people could possibly jump straight into a powerhouse performance unit like this?!" I honestly assumed they’d take an established Kenshuusei unit and absorb them all at once. Then came Hana Kojima. I remembered her covering Juice=Juice’s "More! Mi Amore" at the Momoiro Utagassen—her solo vocals were so rich, grounded, and utterly cool. But when I looked into her back then, I learned her idol group was disbanding, and I remember thinking, "What an incredible talent to go to waste..." So when news broke that she was joining, I was stunned: "Wait, a mid-career hire like this is actually possible?!" As it turned out, former Hello Project member Karin Miyamoto had been advising her, and Hana ended up auditioning through an outside agency to secure her spot. Absolutely unreal events unfolding—and yet, because it’s BEYOOOOONDS, it somehow felt entirely natural. Because honestly, it just works. でも「このパフォーマンス集団にここから合流する人たちってどんな人なん！？」ってなりました。研修生ユニット組んで、ユニットごと加入だと思ってたし。そしたら小島はなさんがやってきて。ももいろ歌合戦でJuice=Juiceの『盛れ！ミ・アモーレ』をカバーしてて、ソロの歌声に芯があってかっこよくて、でもよくよく聞いてみれば所属しているアイドルグループが解散するということで「なんともったいない・・」となっていた方。「こんな中途採用あり得るんですか！？」と思っていたら、ハロプロOGの宮本佳林ちゃんさんが小島はなさんの相談に乗っていて、他事務所からオーディションを受けて加入が決まったと・・とんでもないことが起こってる！けどBEYOOOOONDSだから全然あり！みたいな空気。だってほんとにそうだから。

AMEFURASSHI's performance at Momoiro Uta Gassen was so cool! ももいろ歌合戦でのAMEFURASSHIの出番、かっこいい！

Shortly after, they announced that Mano Otsubo would be promoted from the Kenshuusei, alongside Yuna Sugiyama, who joined through the general auditions. To be honest, there were a lot of unknowns at first, but looking back two months after the graduation concert, all I can say is that they are hands down the absolute best addition to the group. そして続報で、研修生から昇格の大坪茉乃ちゃんとオーディションから一般加入の杉山結菜ちゃんが入ることになった。正直未知数なところが多かったんだけど、卒コンから2ヶ月後に思うことは最高の新メンたちだったってこと。

Hello Pro Kenshusei dreaming of their debut are shining so bright. デビューを夢見る研修生、輝いてる

Even so, when it was announced that these new members would be participating in the Yokohama Arena concert right after their joining was made public, I couldn't bring myself to welcome it with open arms. This was the very first standalone Yokohama Arena show that the current lineup had worked so hard to achieve. It was the exact goal Kurumin had set for the group at the start of the year. So for their very first time on that Arena stage, I desperately wanted it to belong solely to the members who had fought so hard to get there. でも、新メンバー発表の後、この新メンバーも横アリに参加するってアナウンスされたことに対して、わたしは素直に歓迎することができなかったんです。だって今のメンバーが頑張って頑張って掴んだ初めての横浜アリーナの単独公演。くるみんが年始に掲げていた目標でもあったし。だから初めての横アリは、頑張ってきたみんなで立ってほしいという気持ちがあった。

BEYOOOOONDS' reaction when the tour finale at Yokohama Arena was announced. ツアーファイナルが横アリだと発表されたときのビヨちゃん

On the day of the concert, the three of them made their appearance during the interlude video. The concept of this tour was centered around movies and time loops, featuring short acting skits. The interlude took that time-loop theme and ran with it: Kokoro Maeda, playing an office worker who keeps looping through time, runs into three newly hired recruits—the new members—instead of her usual boss, played by Shiori Nishida. That narrative bridge was pure genius. That brief skit was all it took to make me think, "Yep, these girls are officially BEYOOOOONDS now." So by the time they merged into the live performance for That’s LIFE!, the transition felt incredible. I went from "Wait, are they really doing the new member reveal right now?" straight to "Awwwww, they are SO absurdly cute!!" 当日、ライブ中の幕間VTRで3人が登場。今回のライブツアーのコンセプトは映画館で、タイムリープをもとにした寸劇もあって、幕間VTRもタイムリープもの。何度かタイムリープした会社員役の前田こころさんが上司役の西田汐里さんではなく、新入社員の新メンバー3人に会うという流れ。このワンクッションがあってからの『That’s LIFE!』だったんですね。もう、これは完璧な流れだった。VTRでもうすっかりこの子達はもうBEYOOOOONDSだって思えて、そこからの合流してパフォーマンスに参加だったからめちゃくちゃよかったです。「え、新メンのお披露目あるんだ」みたいな思いを持っていたのに「ええええええええ！！かわえええええ、、、かわええええええええ！！」しか言えなくなった。

The new members already getting the full BEYOOOOONDS treatment. 早速BEYOOOOONDS仕様にされる新メンバーの皆さん

Later on, when the behind-the-scenes footage was released and showed the three of them pouring their hearts into dance rehearsals, I was completely sold. I thought, "Yep, I have no choice but to root for these girls with everything I've got." I fell for them completely. The sheer, unfiltered charm of Yuna Sugiyama in the videos shared on BEYOOOOONDS' official X account was insane. And in the YouTube performance of Positive Program, where they weren't just dancing but contributing vocals too, Mano Otsubo’s smile was so dazzlingly "idol" that it hit me like a truck. This addition was, without a shadow of a doubt, a home run. It goes without saying that having them on board has made the world built by the original members shine even brighter. 後から公開された舞台裏の密着映像でこの3人がダンスの練習をしている様子も見ることができたんですけど「この子達まじで全力で応援するしかない」ってなったし。もうメロメロになるしかなかった。BEYOOOOONDSの公式Twitterで共有される杉山結菜ちゃんのあまりに天真爛漫すぎる動画もやばかったし、YouTubeで公開された『ポジティブプログラム』はダンスだけじゃなくて歌唱でも参加してたんですけど、大坪茉乃ちゃんのスマイルがとんでもなく"アイドル"でほんとにすごくて、この補強は完全に大正解だと確信したわけです。新メンバーが加入したことでオリジナルメンバーのお姉さんたちが醸し出す世界がより良いものになってるのはいうまでもないし。

Hearing Kurumi's vocal parts split among the three new members got me crying. くるみんの歌唱パートが3人に割り振られてて泣ける

Their trajectory from here on out is completely wild: their debut stage was a standalone show at Yokohama Arena, their autumn schedule is focused primarily on stage plays rather than a traditional concert tour, and when an autumn live show finally got scheduled, it was kicked off right at the Budokan. It's an utterly unorthodox path. But then again, BEYOOOOONDS has never been a normal idol group—and honestly, that's exactly what makes them so great. I'm looking forward to living through this new era of BEYOOOOONDS. それからこの3人は、初舞台が横アリの単独、秋はライブではなく舞台の公演がメイン、遅れて秋のライブが決まったと思ったら初っ端は武道館公演ということで、通常では考えられない道を歩んでいってるわけなんですね。でもBEYOOOOONDSって普通じゃないアイドルだし、それもまたいいよねって感じ。新生BEYOOOOONDSを楽しみに生きていきたいですね。

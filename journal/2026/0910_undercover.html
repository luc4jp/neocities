Living out my secret agent fantasies with real-life undercover missions.

とっても楽しい潜入捜査

I love diving into worlds I don't know anything about. I actually go to venues and stores, and sign up on websites. One of the most fascinating experiences I've had was infiltrating a MLM seminar for cosmetics. Watching how people get scammed and stripped of their money—while total emptiness is completely covered up and hidden by climbing ranks through buying products and recruiting others, alongside the tight bonds between everyone in the room—is just a masterclass in human psychology. It's wild to hear slides and explanations breaking compliance rules, and to witness psychological manipulation techniques deployed everywhere. わたしは自分の知らない世界へ自分の身をもって潜入するのがすごく好き。会場とかお店とかに実際に足を運ぶし、Webサイトへの登録もする。自分が体験したなかだと化粧品関連のマルチのセミナーが面白かった。こんなふうに人は騙されて、お金を取られていくのね。商品を買ったり勧誘したりして地位を上げていくことや、ここにいる人たち同士の繋がりによって、その虚しさは完全に蓋をされて見えないようになっているのね、と勉強になります。使ってはいけない表現をしているスライドや説明が聞けるのも、人の心の動きを利用したテクニックを使いまくってるのも面白い

The heartbreaking part is that my aunt's family is head-over-heels into this MLM, and through that connection, they're also getting milked dry by a corrective underwear scam. I've already infiltrated that lingerie shop too, and their tactics are insane. I actually witnessed a consultation go down like this: 'Your size has changed a bit~ so I guess we need to upgrade you to a new set (around 250,000 yen). Shall we set up another loan?' Unbelievable. You're signing up for a new loan before the old one is even paid off?! The person taking out that loan was my cousin, by the way. 悲しいのはそのマルチにとってもお熱なのが叔母の家族で、そこの繋がりで補正下着にもお金を搾り取られてるってこと。その補正下着のお店にも潜入済みなんですが、やり口がすごい。「サイズちょっと変わりましたね〜じゃあ新しいセット（25万円くらい）に変えなきゃですね。ローンまた組みましょうか」という接客を見ました。すんんんんごいやり口。ローンを支払い切らないうちに新しいローンを組んじゃうの！？その契約書にサインをしてたのは叔母の娘、つまりわたしのいとこなんですけどね

My mom almost got sucked into it too, and it was a close call. She never even buys Wacoal underwear normally, so why on earth was she ready to drop cash on this?! The day I came back home for a visit happened to be the exact day my mom was heading to the shop to sign the loan. When I suggested, 'Let's go grab some udon,' she looked all fishy and hesitant, so I pressed her and paperwork came tumbling out. I called the shop on her behalf, took her out for udon, and bought her Wacoal underwear at Aeon instead. We got high-quality lingerie for about one-eighth the price of those scam shapewear sets. Afterward, I reached out to my cousin to figure out how things got to this point, and went undercover at the shop myself, hiding the fact that I was the daughter of a customer who refused to sign a loan. うちの母も危うく買いそうになっていて危なかったです。普段ワコールの下着も買わないくせになんでこれにはお金を出そうという気になったんや！帰省した日がちょうど母がローン組みにお店に行く日だったんです。「うどん食べに行こ」って言ったら、なーんかモニョモニョしてるから問いただしたら支払い計画の紙が出てきて。母の代わりに店に連絡を入れて、うどんを食べに行ってイオンでワコールの下着を買いましたけどね。1/8くらいの価格で高品質な下着が買えた。その後にどうしてこうなったのか調べるためにいとこに連絡を取って、ローンを契約しなかった客の娘であることは伏せて潜入したんでした

That signature Seinen manga mix of dark psychological chaos and sheer brutality hits different. 青年誌漫画特有の面白さとグロさで最高

At the seminar, the local MLM bigwig up on stage preached, 'It's crucial to do your own research and become a smart consumer,' but literally nobody in the room is doing it. Nodding along to feel-good buzzwords at a seminar, taking notes, and repeating them to others without ever checking the facts is NOT what 'doing your own research' means.
Rereading *Yamikin Ushijima-kun* made me realize: never assume you're on the smart side, and never stop learning.

セミナーでその地域の胴元が「自分で調べて賢い消費者になるのが大事」って言ってたけどみんなやってないじゃん。セミナーで当たり障りのないことだけを聞いて、頷いて、メモをとって、なんの裏も取らずに人に話すのは「調べる」って言わないよ
